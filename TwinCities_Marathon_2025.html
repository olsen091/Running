Training Notes
Strides (●) — Aug 5 and Aug 11 sessions were labeled with strides in Runalyze. 4–6 × 100m accelerations added after aerobic runs to maintain leg turnover and neuromuscular sharpness.
City of Lakes Half (Sep 7) — Mid-plan tune-up race run as part of a bigger day: warm-up miles, the 13.1-mile race (1:23:29), and a 3-mile cooldown. A strong fitness indicator heading into the final push.
Ripcord 1Ks (Sep 17) — Key interval workout: East Hartford Ripcord 1K repeats, 8.9 miles total. This was the sharpest speed session of the build, two weeks before the key long run week.
Hill Sandwich (Jul 9) — Distinctive early workout mixing hill reps with aerobic miles. Week 13 also included a cross-training day (soccer/football on Jul 13) logged in Runalyze.
